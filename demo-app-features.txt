Demo-App Features

Bug Mode Toggle

Switch to enable/disable intentional bugs for testing automation and AI agents.
Home Screen

Navigation to Login/Profile, Products, Cart, Calculator, and Contact Form screens.
Login/Profile

Login form with username and password.
Bug Mode: Accepts any password, always shows success.
Profile view after login, with logout option.
Products Screen

List of products (Laptop, Phone, Headphones, Tablet).
Select quantity and add products to cart.
Bug Mode: Shows wrong quantity, displays incorrect prices (10% lower).
Shopping Cart 

View cart items, remove items, see total price.
Checkout with confirmation modal.
Bug Mode: Overcharges total by 50%, removes wrong item, checkout skips confirmation.
Calculator

Simple calculator for addition, subtraction, multiplication, division.
Bug Mode: Incorrect calculations (adds/subtracts extra, doubles/halves result).
Contact Form

Email and message input, submit button.
Bug Mode: Accepts any email format, skips validation, always shows success.
Accessibility

All UI elements have stable accessibilityLabel identifiers for reliable automation.
